### Hi, I'm Nirwan

I build AI that people can rely on, in research and in products. I live in Tromsø, Norway.

- **Research.** PhD fellow in artificial intelligence at UiT The Arctic University of Norway
  (2022 – spring 2027). I work on deep learning and computer vision for microscopy: storing
  images compactly, segmenting them, and tracking cells over time.
- **CVQ** ([cvq.no](https://cvq.no)). I founded CVQ in 2026. It helps municipalities run
  public consultations that residents actually take part in: plain-language summaries of
  case documents, residents' views, and a report for the people who decide. Bodø kommune is
  running a test pilot.
- **BrindleBrief** ([brindlebrief.com](https://brindlebrief.com)). I co-founded BrindleBrief,
  an AI assistant that organises the work of small service businesses and gives the owner a
  daily brief.
